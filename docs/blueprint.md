# **App Name**: ElectroCart

## Core Features:

- Ad-Driven Conversion: Streamlined Ad-to-Purchase Flow: Guide users from ads directly to product pages, ensuring a smooth path to checkout with minimal steps.
- Product Discovery: Intuitive Product Browsing: Facilitate easy exploration of electronics through clear category divisions, detailed product listings, and advanced filtering options.
- Product Details: Detailed Product Presentations: Offer comprehensive product information with high-quality image galleries, key features, specifications, and customer reviews to inform purchase decisions.
- Seamless Checkout: Efficient Cart and Checkout: Simplify the shopping cart and checkout process with clear summaries, easy adjustments, secure payment options, and transparent order confirmations.
- Transparent Policies: Policy-Driven Trust: Prominently display clear, accessible policies for shipping, returns, refunds, and warranties to build customer trust in a bootstrapped brand.
- AI-Powered Upselling: Smart Product Recommendations: The app will suggest related products to the user as a tool to increase the liklihood of further purchase by presenting additional options that are relevant to their interests and the items they are currently considering.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft creme (#F5F5DC) to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.
- Background color: Very light creme (#F8F8FF) providing a subtle contrast.
- Accent color: A muted green (#8FBC8F) used sparingly for interactive elements and key highlights, to add a touch of freshness.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for elegant headings. Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clear and readable body text.
- Modern, minimalist icons with a focus on clarity and ease of recognition.
- Clean, grid-based layout that ensures content is well-organized and easy to navigate. Ample white space to avoid clutter and enhance readability.
- Subtle animations and transitions on hover states and page loads to provide a modern and engaging user experience.
